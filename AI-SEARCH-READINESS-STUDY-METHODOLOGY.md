## 1. Study Objective & Research Purpose

The **UBE AI Search Readiness Study 2026** is a proprietary research initiative designed to measure the technical, structured, informational, and authority readiness of independent small and midsize North American business websites for discovery across traditional search engines and AI-powered search channels (including Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT Search, Microsoft Copilot, and Perplexity).

The objective is to establish an empirical, reproducible baseline of web optimization, identifying critical technical bottlenecks, schema gaps, content accessibility hurdles, and authority deficits that impact discovery in answer engines (AEO) and generative search experiences (GEO).

---

## 2. Revised Operational Target Population & Sample Definition

The study does NOT rely on vague assumptions or generic marketing labels. The target population is operationally defined as **Independent small and midsize North American businesses with standalone public websites**.

### Inclusion Criteria
A candidate website must satisfy ALL of the following operational conditions:
1. **Primary Website**: Represents the business's main primary web domain.
2. **Independent Operation**: Operates as an independent business entity.
3. **Public Accessibility**: Accessible publicly via standard HTTP/HTTPS without authentication or paywalls.
4. **Geographic Presence**: Headquartered or actively operating in North America (USA or Canada).
5. **Active Commercial Operations**: Displays active commercial offerings or services during the audit window.

### Operational Exclusion Criteria
Candidate websites are EXCLUDED if they match any of the following:
1. Publicly traded corporations or subsidiaries/business units of enterprise brand conglomerates (e.g. Shutterfly subsidiaries).
2. Fortune 500 companies or major national enterprise chains.
3. Multi-vendor marketplaces representing thousands of independent sellers (e.g. Cratejoy).
4. Pure affiliate, content aggregator, or thin lead-generation directory websites.
5. Government (.gov) or educational (.edu) institutions.
6. Parked, dormant, or under-construction domains.

## 3. Revised 100-Point Scoring Framework (Weighted V2.0)

Based on findings from the Phase 1 Pilot audit, score weightings were rebalanced to increase emphasis on Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) authority evidence while streamlining technical crawler eligibility:

| Category | Description | Measurable Criteria | Max Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Category 1: Technical Search Readiness** | Crawlability, indexability, mobile UX, canonicals, sitemap, HTTP status | 10 Criteria (2.0 pts each) | 20 Points |
| **Category 2: AI Discovery Accessibility** | Crawler accessibility (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot, Bingbot), CDN/WAF rules | 5 Criteria (2.0 pts each) | 10 Points |
| **Category 3: Entity & E-E-A-T** | Organization JSON-LD schema, About page, contact info, real leadership, author attribution, trust policies | 10 Criteria (2.0 pts each) | 20 Points |
| **Category 4: AEO / Content Retrievability** | H1/H2/H3 hierarchy, direct answer blocks, definitions, processes, FAQs, semantic HTML | 10 Criteria (2.0 pts each) | 20 Points |
| **Category 5: GEO / Authority Readiness** | Real case studies, first-party proof, original assets, expert commentary, media mentions, platform profiles, partner links | 10 Criteria (3.0 pts each) | 30 Points |
| **TOTAL CORE SCORE** | **Comprehensive AI Search Readiness Score** | **45 Core Criteria** | **100 Points** |

## 5. Systematic Selection & Selection-Bias Mitigation

To eliminate selection bias and prevent choosing "SEO-forward" brands:
1. **Candidate Discovery Sources**: Domains are identified via public local chamber of commerce directories, regional business registers, industry association member lists, and public geographic category searches.
2. **Pre-Audit Manifest Creation**: All 100 candidate domains and 25 reserve domains are documented in sample-manifest.csv BEFORE any AI-search or readiness scoring begins.
3. **No Replacement for Poor Scores**: Once selected and verified for operational independence, a website CANNOT be replaced due to a low AI SEO score.
4. **Reserve Sample Protocol**: The 25-domain reserve list (5 per cohort) is used ONLY if a candidate domain is offline (HTTP 5xx), requires authentication, or is discovered to be an enterprise subsidiary during audit initialization.

---

## 6. Final Reliability & Quality Control Plan

For the final N = 100 study:
1. **Dual-Audit Subsample**: A minimum of **15 final-sample websites** will be independently audited by a second technical auditor working without access to the primary auditor's score sheet.
2. **Reliability Metrics**: QC reporting will publish:
   - Criterion-level percentage agreement rates
   - Mean absolute category-score differences
   - Mean absolute total-score difference
   - Cohen's kappa for categorical PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL decisions and intraclass correlation (ICC) for continuous total scores where statistically appropriate
3. **No Manufactured Percentages**: Total score variances will NOT be converted into generic "agreement percentages".
