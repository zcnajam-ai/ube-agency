## 1. Founder / Executive Thought-Leadership Post

We spent months auditing 100 independent business websites across 45 criteria for AI search readiness.

The most interesting result wasn't that business websites were inaccessible to AI crawlers.

It was the exact opposite.

All 100 websites in our sample were accessible under evaluated crawler access rules (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot, Bingbot).

Yet when we scored deeper entity signals and authority, the picture changed dramatically:

• Technical Search Readiness averaged 95.85% of available points.
• AEO / Content Retrievability averaged 62.20%.
• GEO / Authority Readiness averaged only 36.53%.

Getting crawled is simply not the same as being AI-search ready.

Three key findings for independent business leaders:

1. 58 of 100 audited sites did not fully meet the Organization schema markup criterion on their homepage.
2. 80 of 100 audited sites lacked first-party research or proprietary data assets.
3. eCommerce / D2C websites led the audit (72.85 points vs. 61.88 for miscellaneous independent sites), driven by GEO authority and content retrievability.

We've published the full research report, methodology, inter-rater reliability data, and public aggregate CSV file:

https://unifiedbrandingexperts.com/research/ai-search-readiness-study-2026
