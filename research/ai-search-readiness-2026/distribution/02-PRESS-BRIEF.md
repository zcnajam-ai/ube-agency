## Executive Overview
Unified Branding Experts audited 100 independent small and midsize North American business websites across 45 technical, entity, content-retrievability, crawler-access, and authority criteria using `SCORING-RUBRIC-V2.0`. The research highlights a central contrast: basic technical search accessibility is nearly universal, but machine-readable entity identity, answer retrievability, and GEO authority remain substantially underdeveloped.

---

## Top 5 Publication Findings

1. **Crawler Access vs. GEO Authority**:  
   All 100 websites audited were effectively accessible under the crawler-access rules evaluated (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot, Bingbot). However, GEO / Authority Readiness averaged only **36.53% of available points** (10.96 / 30.0 Pts).  
   *Disclaimer: Crawler accessibility measures whether audited bots were permitted under robots.txt rules; it does not measure AI indexing, ingestion, or citation.*

2. **Technical Search Readiness Maturity**:  
   Technical Search Readiness averaged **95.85% of available points** (19.17 / 20.0 Pts). Foundational items like HTTPS enforcement, direct HTTP 200 responses, canonical tags, and mobile viewports were mature and prevalent.

3. **Machine Entity Schema Gap**:  
   **58 of the 100 websites audited** did not fully meet the study's Organization JSON-LD schema markup criterion on their homepages. Human trust signals (Contact page 96%, About page 92%) were far more common than machine schema.

4. **Original Data & Proprietary Evidence Deficit**:  
   **80 of the 100 websites audited** did not fully meet the study's Original Data & Proprietary Research criterion, highlighting a primary gap in first-party evidence generation.

5. **Cohort Score Decomposition**:  
   Among the five 20-site cohorts in the study, eCommerce / D2C recorded the highest average total score (**72.85 Pts**), leading Miscellaneous Independent (**61.88 Pts**) by 10.97 points. Score decomposition proves this gap was driven primarily by GEO authority (+5.32 Pts) and AEO content retrievability (+2.85 Pts), rather than foundational technical differences (+0.65 Pts).

## Study Methodology & Sample Boundaries
- **Sample Size**: N = 100 independent SMB websites across North America (19 Northeast US, 21 South US, 15 Midwest US, 29 West US, 16 Canada).
- **Cohort Structure**: 5 cohorts (eCommerce, Professional/Local, B2B, Health/Wellness, Miscellaneous Independent; n=20 per cohort).
- **Scoring Rubric**: `SCORING-RUBRIC-V2.0` (45 criteria across 5 categories; total score range 0–100 Pts).
- **Inter-Rater Reliability**: 15-site double audit yielded 100% criterion decision agreement (Kappa = 1.00, ICC = 1.00, 0.0 Pts total score delta).
- **Limitations**: Constructed stratified quota sample; ceiling effect observed in Category 2 due to default wildcard permissions; evaluates publicly observable signals rather than unobservable internal LLM weights.
