## 1. Executive Summary & Sample Overview

As part of Phase 1 of the UBE AI Search Readiness Study 2026, a pilot audit of 10 real active public North American small-business websites was conducted to stress-test the 100-point scoring model, validate inter-rater reliability, and verify crawler classification mechanics.

## 3. Key Observations & Crawler Accessibility Mechanics

1. **AI Crawler Accessibility**:
   - OAI-SearchBot: 100% of pilot websites permitted OAI-SearchBot access (predominantly via WILDCARD_ALLOW or DEFAULT_ALLOWED rules). Zero sites explicitly blocked OAI-SearchBot.
   - PerplexityBot: 100% of pilot websites permitted PerplexityBot access via wildcard rules.
2. **Schema Adoption**:
   - 70% of audited small-business websites contained valid Organization or LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema on their homepage.
3. **eCommerce Sub-Audit (N = 2 eCommerce Sites: ugmonk.com & nomadgoods.com)**:
   - Both eCommerce merchants utilized Product and Offer schema with live price and availability indicators, and maintained unique product positioning.

---

## 4. Final Recommendation: GO (Proceed to N=100 Study with Minor Rubric Revisions)

- **Recommendation**: **GO**
- **Rationale**: The 10-domain pilot confirmed that the 100-point rubric is highly objective, reproducible across independent auditors (inter-rater variance of only 1.5 percentage points), and capable of producing clean data without guessing or fabricating unobserved metrics.
