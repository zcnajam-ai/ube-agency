## 1. Rubric Evaluation & Sampling Lessons

1. **AI Discovery Accessibility**:
   - The pilot produced 15/15 AI Discovery scores across all 10 sites.
   - Bot accessibility functions primarily as a technical prerequisite rather than a market differentiator. Category weighting was rebalanced to 10 points for the final study.
   - Clarified that wildcard allow (User-agent: * Allow: /) provides equal crawl access as explicit bot naming (User-agent: OAI-SearchBot).

2. **Sampling & Population Exclusions**:
   - Spoonflower: Identified as a subsidiary of Shutterfly (Enterprise conglomerate). Flagged as unsuitable for the independent small-business sample.
   - Cratejoy: Identified as a multi-vendor platform marketplace representing thousands of merchants. Flagged as unsuitable as a representation of an individual small-business storefront.
   - Action: Both pilot audits remain preserved in historical validation logs. The final N = 100 study starts fresh with 100 newly verified candidate domains.

3. **GEO / Authority Criteria Expansion**:
   - Replaced unobservable "Quality Backlink Profile" with Verified Third-Party Platform Profiles, Professional/Industry Association Presence, and Verified Partner References.
